I upgraded to the new Retina MBPs and have been loving it so far (minus the random logouts that I’ve now since fixed, or so it seems).

I have a few new purchases to make, such as a 17″ FSI monitor, a 6×6 village blackout tent, and a HDLinkPro with LiveGrade (why does everything have to cost so much?!).

The idea of thunderbolt seemed pretty awesome to be, but I didn’t know what was feasible in terms of compatibility and such, until now.

Here’s my setup:

15.4″ MBP, [LaCie TB eSata Hub](http://www.lacie.com/products/product.htm?id=10574 "LaCie eSata hub"), TB cables, [Sonnet Echo Express PRO express34 adapter](http://sonnettech.com/product/echoexpresscard34thunderbolt.html), and that’s pretty much it.  I’m getting about 180mb/s read speed, which is pretty awesome.  I can download a 64gb card in about 8 minutes to an eSata enabled hard drive, which is awesome seeing how before from PCIe to fw800 I was doing about 24 minutes. It’ll do a checksum and copy to two different drives in about 15 min, that’s fast.

I was having some issues with my [Sonnet TEMPO SATA card](http://sonnettech.com/product/tempo_sata_express34.html), getting it to work with my Echo PRO (as I’m still waiting on my [USB3.0 Red Station](http://www.red.com/store/products/red-station-redmag-18-mini-usb3)) but I just ended up swapping out harddrives for each transfer and that seemed to work well.
