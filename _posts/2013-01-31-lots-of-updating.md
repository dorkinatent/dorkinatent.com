It’s been a busy past 6 months! I haven’t really had time to sleep let alone update this blog, so my apologies!

Once [The Inevitable Defeat of Mister & Pete](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt2113075/) wrapped I immediately went into a 7 week commercial for Exxon Mobil (yes 7 weeks, it was insane).  We traveled across the country via private jet and filmed in 7 different cities totaling over 76 HOURS of footage.

[![Screen Shot 2013-01-31 at 10.05.11 AM]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/lots-of-updating-screen-shot-2013-01-31-at-10-05-11-am.png)]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/lots-of-updating-screen-shot-2013-01-31-at-10-05-11-am.png)

It was a TON of work as I had to keep up with 2 Alexas shooting constantly as well as a C300 (which was eventually dropped and a 3rd Alexa was added instead).  There were days where we shot over 800GB of footage, which is an insane amount of footage.

That job allowed me to upgrade a few things and so I purchased PVWM-2541 as my main coloring monitor, and I have to say once you start working with OLED you never want to go back to LCD.
