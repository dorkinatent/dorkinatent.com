Drillcore is a game by Charlie Anderson. This page explains what the game collects, why, and what you can do about it.

## The short version

Drillcore has no accounts, no ads and no tracking across other apps or websites. Your progress is saved on your device. If you agree to share play data, the game sends us a record of how you play, with nothing in it that names you. You can turn that off at any time.

## Play data (only if you say yes)

The first time you open Drillcore, it asks whether you want to share play data. If you choose SHARE, the game sends a log of what happens in the game:

- how deep each run goes, how long it lasts and how it ends
- what you buy and research, and the choices you make during a run
- which tips you saw, when you paused, and how long you were away from the game
- the game's version and build, and the platform (iOS)

Each log is tagged with a random ID that the game creates on your device. That ID isn't linked to your name, your Apple Account, your Game Center profile or your device's advertising identifier. The log doesn't include your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or anything you type.

We use play data only to find out where the game is confusing, too slow or unfair, and to fix it. We don't sell it or share it with advertisers, and we don't use it to target you.

If you choose NO THANKS, or turn SHARE PLAY DATA off later in Settings, the game stops sending. Nothing recorded while sharing was off is ever sent.

Play data is stored on Cloudflare, which runs the server that receives it. When your device sends the data, Cloudflare handles your IP address to deliver it, as any web server does. We don't store IP addresses with play data.

We keep play data for 12 months, then delete it.

## Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, the game sends your scores and achievements to Apple's Game Center. Apple's privacy policy covers that data. We see only what Game Center shows on its leaderboards.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device, such as when research finishes. They're scheduled on your device and don't send anything to us.

## TestFlight

If you're testing a beta build through TestFlight, Apple shares some information with us, such as crash reports, the feedback and screenshots you send, and the email address you used to join (if you were invited by email). Apple's TestFlight terms cover that information.

## Children

Drillcore isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Your choices

- Turn SHARE PLAY DATA off in Settings at any time.
- Delete the app to remove your saved progress and the local play log from your device.
- Ask us to delete play data you already sent. Because the data has no name on it, we need its ID to find it: Settings shows your play data ID under SHARE PLAY DATA. Email that ID to [charlie@dorkinatent.com](mailto:charlie@dorkinatent.com) and we'll delete everything sent under it within 30 days.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects what we collect, the game will ask again before it sends anything new.
