Useful links for the creative process.  Feel free to email me suggestions and I’ll update this as needed.

**CAMERA RELATED:**

<http://www.red.com/tools/crop-factor> – Use this to figure out the crop factor with different red sensors and what not

<http://www.arri.com/camera/alexa/tools/arri_frameline_composer/> – Create framelines as needed for ANY Arri camera (I use this almost on every job)

Motion Picture Lens Database – http://bit.ly/2uJ7cI2

**SOFTWARE:**

<http://pomfort.com/> – Company who creates LiveGrade & Silverstack

<http://www.divergentmedia.com/scopebox> – Video monitoring and capture software (what I currently use to capture stills)

<http://mikasky.free.fr/foolcolor/> – macOS / iOS control of Red cameras, works great with the WLAN setup in [this](http://dorkinatent.com/2016/03/18/setting-up-the-teradek-colr-to-work-with-the-bullet-m2/) post

**HARDWARE:**

<https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/support/> – Software & Drivers for many Blackmagic devices

**MISC.:**

<http://www.rtings.com/> – Calibration settings for any make & model of TV

<http://training.abelcine.com/phantom-technicians-list/> – List of Phantom Techs authorized by AbelCine, also lets you schedule training to be on this list (HIGHLY recommend if you are a DIT)

<http://www.xrite.com/hue-test> – Test yourself to make sure you are not color deficient, or realize how to adjust if you are 😉

<http://www.brucelindbloom.com/> – Anything you want to technically know about color

<http://digitalfactbook.tv/> – Learn the terminology of an every growing world of terminologies

<http://acescentral.com> – Anything you’d ever want to know about ACES

<http://liftgammagain.com/forum/index.php?forums/dailies-workflow.10/> – Forum for different topics on dailies workflow
