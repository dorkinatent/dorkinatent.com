While I was waiting on some people to get back to me for a checkout for an upcoming TV show I decided to put together a little program to help with creating default folder structures that I typically use, so now I’m making that available to everyone else as well!

Here’s how it works:

[![Screen Shot 2015-05-08 at 1.42.22 PM](/assets/images/dit-folder-structure-screen-shot-2015-05-08-at-1-42-22-pm.png)](/assets/images/dit-folder-structure-screen-shot-2015-05-08-at-1-42-22-pm.png)

You double click the icon, and it will ask you for a day.  I tend to use the date in the fashion of year, month, day, but you can modify it to be whatever you want, such as DAY01, DAY25, etc.

It will default to the desktop, so you should choose where you want this to go by hitting OTHER and then pointing to what destination you want the folder structure to populate.

[![Screen Shot 2015-05-08 at 1.42.30 PM](/assets/images/dit-folder-structure-screen-shot-2015-05-08-at-1-42-30-pm.png)](/assets/images/dit-folder-structure-screen-shot-2015-05-08-at-1-42-30-pm.png)

[![Screen Shot 2015-05-08 at 1.42.51 PM](/assets/images/dit-folder-structure-screen-shot-2015-05-08-at-1-42-51-pm.png)](/assets/images/dit-folder-structure-screen-shot-2015-05-08-at-1-42-51-pm.png)

Then it will ask you for a camera name.

[![Screen Shot 2015-05-08 at 1.43.01 PM](/assets/images/dit-folder-structure-screen-shot-2015-05-08-at-1-43-01-pm.png)](/assets/images/dit-folder-structure-screen-shot-2015-05-08-at-1-43-01-pm.png)

Such as the F55, Epic, Alexa, etc.

[![Screen Shot 2015-05-08 at 1.43.11 PM](/assets/images/dit-folder-structure-screen-shot-2015-05-08-at-1-43-11-pm.png)](/assets/images/dit-folder-structure-screen-shot-2015-05-08-at-1-43-11-pm.png)

Once you hit continue, it will automatically populate all the folder structures and all that jazz

[![Screen Shot 2015-05-08 at 1.43.22 PM](/assets/images/dit-folder-structure-screen-shot-2015-05-08-at-1-43-22-pm.png)](/assets/images/dit-folder-structure-screen-shot-2015-05-08-at-1-43-22-pm.png) [![Screen Shot 2015-05-08 at 1.43.31 PM](/assets/images/dit-folder-structure-screen-shot-2015-05-08-at-1-43-31-pm.png)](/assets/images/dit-folder-structure-screen-shot-2015-05-08-at-1-43-31-pm.png) [![Screen Shot 2015-05-08 at 1.43.36 PM](/assets/images/dit-folder-structure-screen-shot-2015-05-08-at-1-43-36-pm.png)](/assets/images/dit-folder-structure-screen-shot-2015-05-08-at-1-43-36-pm.png)
