Anyhoo, here’s the graphical layout of how I have my ins and outs for my cart.  I use a [Blackmagic Smart Videohub](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/smartvideohub) for all my routing needs (I’m currently working on making a mac app that acts as a Macro switcher…more on that later, it’s taking some time to develop).

You’ll see that I take a log image from each camera and then send that out to my IS-Minis, and then send that back into my router.  The reason for this is so that I can quickly show LOG whenever the DP asks for it.
